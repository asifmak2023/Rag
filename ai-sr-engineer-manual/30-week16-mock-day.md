# 30. Mock interview day (week 16, or any Saturday)

Do this once with a timer. Phone in another room. Standing for the
talks. Laptop only for the coding block.

## 09:00 HR (15 min)

Answer out loud:

- Walk me through your CV.
- Why this role, why onsite, why not remote.
- Current salary, expected, notice.
- Tell me a conflict with an engineer.

Record yourself. Cut filler words.

## 09:20 Design (20 min)

Coin flip: bank loans, factory camera, ticket router (chapter 18).
8 minutes talk, 12 minutes they interrupt you (pause the recording
and ask yourself hostile questions: "why not GPT?", "why not 99%
accuracy?", "we only have 600 rows").

## 09:45 Coding (45 min)

Closed book.

Option A: pandas + Pipeline + F1 on a tiny CSV you wrote.
Option B: PyTorch MLP loop on random tensors, print val acc.
Option C: FastAPI predict stub + Pydantic, no model, fake return.

Rotate A/B/C across three Saturdays.

## 10:30 Debug (20 min)

Open `28-debugging-playbook.md` after you try. Before: take a lab,
break it, fix it.

## 10:50 Capstone demo (10 min)

`docker run` or uvicorn + curl. Then `report.md` in 5 minutes.

## 11:00 Post-mortem (20 min)

Write:

- Questions you stalled on
- Numbers you did not have (salary)
- Code you had to look up

That list is next week's drills.

## Pass / fail for the job

Fail: you still say "I will learn on the job" for sklearn or Docker.
Pass: you show the API, the metric, the leak you refused, and you
can start Monday at Bahria Complex 1.
