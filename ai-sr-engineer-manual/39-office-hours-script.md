# 39. What to say in the room (scripts)

Memorize structure, not a play. Fill blanks with truth.

## "Tell me about yourself" (90s)

Chapter 25. Stop after onsite sentence.

## "Why Pivotize / why AI now?"

"I already build software and numerical systems. The missing piece was
shipping models as products. I spent a structured conversion on sklearn,
PyTorch, and deployment. I want to do that in a team that sits together,
which is why onsite in Karachi works for me."

## "You do not have 4 years of PyTorch"

"Correct. I have years of C/C++, MATLAB, SQL, and software engineering.
PyTorch and sklearn are recent and evidenced by two repositories I can
open right now. I will not invent dates."

If they require a literal 4 years of DL only, this may not be your
offer. Do not panic-lie.

## "Walk through a project"

Capstone A only:

1. Decision the score changes
2. Grain and leaks refused
3. Baseline vs GBDT
4. Threshold from recall floor
5. API fields and version
6. How it fails (drift, contract change)
7. Next improvement

## "Why not deep learning here?"

"4000 tabular rows, mixed types, need an answer this week. GBDT. I
use nets when the input is raw image or text and I have enough data
or a pretrained backbone."

## "How do you stay current?"

"One paper or release note a week, written in a template: problem,
method, whether it changes our stack. I do not reimplement papers for
sport."

## Hostile

"Accuracy is 99%." -> "Show the class rate and the PR curve."
"Just use GPT." -> "For a 200 ms loan score with an audit trail, no."
"Can you work from home?" -> "The role is onsite. I can do that."

## End of interview

"I can start after [notice]. I prefer to spend week one on data
access and a baseline, not a greenfield neural net."

Then thank them. Leave.
